What is a narrative device?
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
A storytelling technique used for an overall story framing (e.g., first person perspective) or specific story features (e.g., plot twist).

What is picaresque?
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
A genre where a character of low social standing navigates through situations relying on wit.
